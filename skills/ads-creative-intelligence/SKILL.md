# Ads and Creative Intelligence

1. Use `ads.search_public` for public ad-transparency discovery.
2. Inspect advertiser landing pages with `web.search`, `web.history` and `competitive.snapshot`.
3. Classify hooks, offers, proof, CTA, audience and funnel stage.
4. Identify repeated patterns and differentiation opportunities.

This is public-surface intelligence, not a licensed ad firehose. State coverage limitations.

## Nexus MCP tools used
`ads.search_public`, `web.search`, `web.history`, `competitive.snapshot`
