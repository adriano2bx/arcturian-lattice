# Content Gap Intelligence

1. Define target customer problems and commercial themes.
2. Use `seo.serp` and `web.search` to map current search coverage.
3. Use `web.sitemap` on key competitors to inventory their content footprint.
4. Sample high-value YouTube content with `youtube.transcript`.
5. Use `social.search_mentions` and `news.search` to identify questions and emerging themes.
6. Compare competitors with `competitive.compare`.
7. Prioritize gaps by buyer relevance, commercial intent, differentiation and evidence of demand.

Do not invent keyword volume. Use observed ranking/content evidence and clearly state demand proxies.

## Nexus MCP tools used
`seo.serp`, `web.sitemap`, `web.search`, `youtube.transcript`, `social.search_mentions`, `news.search`, `competitive.compare`
